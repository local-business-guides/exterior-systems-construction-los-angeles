LOS ANGELES, California (November 8, 2026) Exterior Systems Construction has released a guide to best water intrusion testing in Los Angeles, along with a related YouTube video, podcast episode and audio version on SoundCloud. The materials are for homeowners, HOAs, property managers and commercial clients considering an evaluation when moisture appears in a building.

The guide discusses why an assessment should begin by understanding where moisture is showing up and how water may be entering. It also covers the value of considering the affected area, the parts of the property involved and any repairs made before deciding what evaluation or testing is appropriate. Those details can help property owners and managers discuss the issue with a contractor and plan next steps.

Exterior Systems Construction provides assessment and water intrusion testing to investigate moisture entry before recommending work. Its services include roof repairs and reroofing, waterproofing, balcony and stair repairs, drainage improvements, stucco repairs and protective coatings. Building envelope testing, moisture mapping and scheduled inspections can support repair planning for single-family homes, multi-unit buildings and managed properties.

The guide may be useful to Los Angeles property owners and managers who are seeing signs of moisture or reviewing an ongoing leak concern. Reviewing the affected area and previous repair history can help frame a conversation about an appropriate evaluation and testing scope. The related video is available on YouTube, and the audio version is on SoundCloud. The guide and podcast episode were also released this week. Links and the podcast platform were not included in the announcement details.

To discuss a building concern, contact Exterior Systems Construction with information about the affected area, previous repairs and the evaluation or testing scope under consideration. A phone number was not provided with the announcement.
