Questions to ask before agreeing on testing

A clear discussion helps you understand what the proposed evaluation is intended to investigate. Ask what area will be assessed, what details the team needs from you and how the findings may inform repair planning. If you’ve received suggestions for different repairs, share them so the conversation can account for what has already been considered.

Use this short preparation list:

  • Identify the specific room, wall or exterior feature involved
  • Note when the issue began and whether it has changed
  • Collect available records of earlier inspections or repairs
  • Ask what testing or assessment is being proposed and why
  • Confirm what access may be needed at the property

Avoid choosing an evaluation based on the lowest-sounding scope alone. A scope that leaves out the area tied to the moisture concern may not answer the question you need addressed. On the other hand, don’t assume every property needs every type of testing. Ask for an explanation of how the suggested work relates to your report.
