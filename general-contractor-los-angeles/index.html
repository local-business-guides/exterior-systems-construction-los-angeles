Define the exterior problem first

When searching for a general contractor Los Angeles homeowners can contact about exterior work, start by listing what needs attention. Avoid deciding on a repair based only on a stain or crack, since the visible damage may not explain the full issue.

Work that may be part of the scope

Exterior projects can involve several types of repair and planning. Services include roof repairs and reroofing, waterproofing, balcony and stair repairs, drainage improvements, stucco repairs and protective coatings. Building envelope testing, moisture mapping and scheduled inspections can support planning for repairs at homes and managed properties.

Before a visit, prepare a short checklist:

  • Identify the locations where you see damage or moisture
  • Note when the issue began and how it has changed
  • Gather records or details about previous repairs
  • Consider access to affected rooms and exterior areas
  • List questions about the evaluation or testing scope

One mistake to avoid is asking for a specific fix before explaining the full history. Another is overlooking nearby affected areas when describing the job. If moisture is spreading, finishes are deteriorating or exterior components appear damaged, contact a professional to discuss an assessment rather than waiting for the symptoms to become clearer.
